Commission Information for Providers

For every successful service completed through the platform, a 15% commission will be applied to the total service charge.

For Cash Payments: You will collect the full service fee from the client directly. The platform commission (15%) for cash payments will be debited from your registered mobile money account (MTN MoMo/Airtel Money) at the end of each day/week, or you will be required to remit it directly via mobile money transfer to the platform's designated account.

For Digital Payments (MoMo, Airtel Money, Card): The commission will be automatically deducted from the service fee before the remaining amount is paid out to your registered mobile money account.

Detailed payment statements and reconciliation will be available in your provider dashboard (coming soon!).
